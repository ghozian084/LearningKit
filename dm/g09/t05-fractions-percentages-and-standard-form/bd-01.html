Meeting 1: Fraction Language (5.1)

Main Concept Types of Fractions

  • Proper: top < bottom, e.g. $\frac{3}{4}$
  • Improper: top ≥ bottom, e.g. $\frac{7}{4}$
  • Mixed number: whole + fraction, e.g. $1\frac{3}{4}$
  • Equivalent: $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{4}{8}$

Convert

Mixed → Improper: $2\frac{3}{4} = \frac{2 \times 4 + 3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}$

Improper → Mixed: $\frac{17}{5} = 17 \div 5 = 3$ remainder $2 = 3\frac{2}{5}$

Try It

Meeting 1: Multiplying & Dividing (5.2)

Multiply Top × Top, Bottom × Bottom

📄 Paper folding: fold $\frac{3}{4}$, then shade $\frac{2}{3}$ of it.

$\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$

Divide Keep, Change, Flip

$\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{3}{8} = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{3} = \frac{24}{12} = 2$

⚠️ Convert mixed numbers to improper fractions first!

Meeting 2: Percentage Change in Health Data

Main Concept

$\text{Percentage change} = \dfrac{\text{Difference}}{\text{Original}} \times 100\%$

One quantity as a % of another: $\dfrac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}} \times 100\%$ (same units!)

Before & After ❤️ Jogging for a month

Resting heart rate drops from 85 bpm to 68 bpm.

Difference $= 85 - 68 = 17$  →  $\frac{17}{85} \times 100\% = 20\%$ decrease

Meeting 2: The Micro & the Macro (5.4)

Critical Thinking 🦠 vs 🇮🇩

Covid-19 virus width: 0.000 000 12 m

Indonesia's population: 275 000 000

How do scientists write these without miscounting zeros?

Standard Form $A \times 10^n$,  $1 \le A < 10$

2.75000000 → $2.75 \times 10^{8}$ (point jumps 8 places → n = +8)
0.0000001.2 → $1.2 \times 10^{-7}$ (point jumps 7 places → n = −7)

🐸 Count the frog jumps of the decimal point: big numbers → $n$ positive, tiny numbers → $n$ negative.

Meeting 2: Calculating in Standard Form

Index Laws

Multiply: $(2 \times 10^3) \times (4 \times 10^5) = 8 \times 10^{3+5} = 8 \times 10^8$

Divide: $(6 \times 10^8) \div (3 \times 10^2) = 2 \times 10^{8-2} = 2 \times 10^6$

Fix $A$: $(9 \times 10^4)(5 \times 10^3) = 45 \times 10^7 = 4.5 \times 10^8$
