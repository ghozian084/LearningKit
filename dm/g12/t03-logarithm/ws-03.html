💡 Pro Tips for Solving Log Equations

1. Compress First: Use the Log Laws (+ means multiply, - means divide) to compress multiple logs into a single logarithm on each side.
2. Convert to Exponent: If you have $\log_a x = y$, convert it to $a^y = x$.
3. CAUTION (Extraneous Roots): You CANNOT take the logarithm of a negative number or zero. Always substitute your final $x$ values back into the original equation and reject any that cause a negative log!
