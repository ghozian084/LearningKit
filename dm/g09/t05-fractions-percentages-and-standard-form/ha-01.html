📄 Paper-Folding Multiplication Guide: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4}$

✂️
  1. Fold a square paper into 4 equal vertical strips. Shade 3 strips — that is $\frac{3}{4}$.
  2. Fold the paper into 3 equal horizontal strips. Shade 2 of them with a different colour — that is $\frac{2}{3}$ of the paper.
  3. Count the boxes shaded twice (top × top = $2 \times 3 = 6$) out of all boxes (bottom × bottom = $3 \times 4 = 12$).
  4. $\frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$ ✅

Project Launch Media: Group Mission Cards

Hand one card to each group in Meeting 1 — glue it on the front of the A3 portfolio

✂️

🟢 Group 1: The Dietary Planners

  • Fractions: Scale a salad using $1\frac{3}{4}$ cups of spinach from 4 people to 18 people.
  • Percentages: Daily sugar drops from 65 g to 25 g. Find the percentage decrease.
  • Standard Form: Write the size of a Vitamin C molecule in standard form and compare it with global annual Vitamin C production (kg).

🔵 Group 2: The Epidemiologists

  • Fractions: $\frac{5}{8}$ of hospital beds are occupied. 120 new beds are added. What fraction is occupied now?
  • Percentages: Flu cases fall from 4 500 to 1 200. Find the percentage decrease.
  • Standard Form: Write the influenza virus (0.000 000 1 m) and global population (8 100 000 000) in standard form. How many viruses laid end-to-end span the equator?

🔴 Group 3: The Fitness Coaches

  • Fractions: A runner completes $3\frac{1}{2}$ laps of $\frac{2}{5}$ km. Find the total distance.
  • Percentages: Resting heart rate improves from 78 bpm to 62 bpm. Find the percentage decrease.
  • Standard Form: A heart beats 35 000 000 times a year. Write this in standard form and find the beats in an 80-year lifespan.

🟡 Group 4: The Hydration Specialists

  • Fractions: A $15\frac{1}{2}$ L dispenser fills $\frac{3}{4}$ L bottles. How many full bottles?
  • Percentages: Goal 2.5 L of water a day; 800 ml drunk by noon. What percentage of the goal is that?
  • Standard Form: One body has $3.7 \times 10^{13}$ cells. How many cells in a class of 30 students?

📋 Portfolio Checklist (glue under the mission card)

✂️ ☐ Page 1 — Scaled Healthy Recipe: original recipe, scale factors for 50 and 3 people, every ingredient worked out as a mixed number.
☐ Page 2 — Health Improvement Analysis: before & after data, multiplier or formula shown, % change with "increase/decrease".
☐ Page 3 — Micro/Macro Data Chart: 5 huge + 5 tiny values, all in $A \times 10^n$ with $1 \le A < 10$, ranked smallest → largest.
