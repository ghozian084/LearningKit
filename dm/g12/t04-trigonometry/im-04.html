📝 Area Two Ways

Triangle: equal sides 1, angle $2\theta$ between them → area $= \frac12 \times 1 \times 1 \times \sin 2\theta$.
Halves: each half is a right triangle with hypotenuse 1 → legs $\sin\theta$ (half the base) and $\cos\theta$ (the altitude).
Rectangle: the two halves fill a $\sin\theta \times \cos\theta$ rectangle → area $= \sin\theta\cos\theta$.
Same paper, same area: $\frac12 \sin 2\theta = \sin\theta\cos\theta$, so $\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta$ $\blacksquare$
