📝 Step-by-Step Example

Problem: The recipe needs $1\frac{1}{2}$ cups of flour for 4 people. How much for 30 people?

Step 1: Scale factor $= \frac{30}{4} = \frac{15}{2}$
Step 2: Change the mixed number to improper: $1\frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{2}$
Step 3: Multiply: $\frac{3}{2} \times \frac{15}{2} = \frac{45}{4}$
Step 4: Convert back: $\frac{45}{4} = 11\frac{1}{4}$ cups of flour

📝 Step-by-Step Example: Keep, Change, Flip

Problem: 💧 A dispenser holds $15\frac{1}{2}$ L. Each bottle holds $\frac{3}{4}$ L. How many full bottles?

Step 1: Improper fraction first: $15\frac{1}{2} = \frac{31}{2}$
Step 2: Keep, Change, Flip: $\frac{31}{2} \div \frac{3}{4} = \frac{31}{2} \times \frac{4}{3}$
Step 3: Multiply: $\frac{124}{6} = \frac{62}{3} = 20\frac{2}{3}$
Step 4: Only full bottles count → 20 bottles (with $\frac{2}{3}$ of a bottle left over).

📝 Step-by-Step Example: Multiplying in Standard Form

Problem: ❤️ A heart beats $3.5 \times 10^7$ times a year. How many times in 80 years?

Step 1: Write 80 in standard form: $8 \times 10^1$
Step 2: Multiply the numbers, add the powers: $(3.5 \times 8) \times 10^{7+1} = 28 \times 10^8$
Step 3: $28$ is not between 1 and 10! $28 = 2.8 \times 10^1$
Step 4: $2.8 \times 10^1 \times 10^8 = 2.8 \times 10^9$ heartbeats
