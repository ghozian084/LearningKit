The question says…ThinkMultiply byHealth example
"20% less" / "decrease by 20%"$100\% - 20\% = 80\%$0.8030 g sugar → $0.80 \times 30 = 24$ g
"reduce by 35%"$100\% - 35\% = 65\%$0.652000 ml soda → 1300 ml
"5% decrease"$100\% - 5\% = 95\%$0.9580 kg → 76 kg
"increase by 15%"$100\% + 15\% = 115\%$1.151800 kcal → 2070 kcal
"8% more"$100\% + 8\% = 108\%$1.0850 min exercise → 54 min
"find 15% of"$15\% = \frac{15}{100}$0.1515% of 2000 kcal = 300 kcal
"what percentage is A of B?"$\frac{A}{B} \times 100\%$ (same units!)—800 ml of 2.5 L → $\frac{800}{2500} = 32\%$
"percentage change"$\frac{\text{New} - \text{Original}}{\text{Original}} \times 100\%$—85 → 68 bpm: 20% decrease
