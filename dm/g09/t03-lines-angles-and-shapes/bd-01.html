Meeting 1: Lines & Angles

Main Concept Transversals

  • Parallel Lines: Never meet.
  • Transversal: A line cutting across parallel lines.
  • F-Shape: Corresponding Angles (Equal).
  • Z-Shape: Alternate Interior (Equal).
  • C-Shape: Co-interior (Sum to $180^\circ$).

Activity Washi Tape Desks

Hook: "What happens if a weaver's thread crosses parallel lines diagonally?"

Task: Tape two parallel lines and a transversal. (Use the digital Protractor tool above to measure angles on the screen!)

Meeting 1: Transversal Practice

Sample Question Angle Rules

Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. If one acute angle is $40^\circ$, what is the size of its corresponding angle?

Exit Ticket

Draw two parallel lines and a transversal using the Line Tool. If one angle is $110^\circ$, find the alternate interior angle.

Meeting 2: Triangles

Main Concept Triangle Properties

  • Sum of Interior: Always $180^\circ$.
  • Isosceles: 2 equal sides, 2 equal base angles.
  • Exterior Angle Theorem: Exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles.

Activity Paper Tearing Proof

Hook: "Why are Rumah Gadang roofs built as triangles?"

Task: Draw a triangle using the Triangle Tool. Imagine tearing off the 3 corners and aligning them on a straight line. They form $180^\circ$!

Meeting 2: Triangle Angles

Exit Ticket

Meeting 3: Quadrilaterals

Main Concept Shape Properties

  • Parallelogram: Opposite sides parallel/equal. Diagonals bisect.
  • Rhombus: Squashed square. Diagonals cross at $90^\circ$.
  • Kite: Adjacent sides equal. One line of symmetry.

Activity Constructing Shapes

Hook: "How do we ensure a Layang-layang (Kite) flies perfectly straight?"

Task: Use the shape tools to draw a Rectangle. Then draw what it looks like if you 'squash' it into a Parallelogram.

Meeting 4: Polygons

Main Concept Polygon Formulas

  • Interior Angle Sum: $(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$
  • Exterior Angles: ALWAYS sum to $360^\circ$.
  • One Interior + One Exterior = $180^\circ$.

Activity Triangle Splitting

Hook: "Why do bees love hexagonal paving blocks?"

Task: Draw a polygon. Pick ONE vertex and draw diagonals to split it into triangles. Find the $(n-2)$ pattern!

Meeting 5: Circles & Theorems

Main Concept Circle Theorems

  • Semicircle: Angle in a semicircle is $90^\circ$.
  • Center: Angle at center is TWICE the angle at circumference.
  • Cyclic Quads: Opposite angles sum to $180^\circ$.

Activity Thales' Discovery

Hook: "Look at the geometry of this Gamelan Gong."

Task: Draw a circle. Draw a diameter line. Pick a point on the edge and draw lines to the diameter. Use the Protractor tool to measure the $90^\circ$ angle!
