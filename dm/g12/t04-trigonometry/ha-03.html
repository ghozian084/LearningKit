Hands-on 3 · Meeting 3: Cut-and-Rearrange Double Angle Proof

Grade 12 · Topic 3 · EXPLORE phase — one sheet per student

Steps: ➊ Cut out triangle ①. ➋ Cut it along the red altitude into two right triangles (each has hypotenuse 1 and angle $\theta$). ➌ Rotate one half and fit both into rectangle ②. ➍ Compare the two area calculations below.
📐 Area two ways

Triangle ①:   Area $= \frac12 \times 1 \times 1 \times \sin 2\theta = \frac12\sin 2\theta$

Rectangle ②:   Area $= \sin\theta \times \cos\theta$   — but the rectangle is made of the same two halves as the triangle!

So $\frac12\sin 2\theta = \sin\theta\cos\theta$,   which gives   $\boxed{\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta}$

Check with a calculator for $\theta = 25^\circ$:   $\sin 50^\circ =$ __________    $2\sin 25^\circ\cos 25^\circ =$ __________
