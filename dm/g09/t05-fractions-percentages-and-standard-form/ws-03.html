💡 EXPLAIN · $A \times 10^n$, where $1 \le A < 10$15 min
🧠 Rules

➊ Into standard form: move the point until $1 \le A < 10$; count the hops for $n$.   $6\,400\,000 = 6.4 \times 10^6$,   $0.0035 = 3.5 \times 10^{-3}$

➋ Out of standard form: positive $n$ → the number gets bigger; negative $n$ → the number gets smaller.   $2.1 \times 10^{-2} = 0.021$

💡 EXPLAIN · Calculating with the Index LawsShow every step

$(a \times 10^m) \times (b \times 10^n) = (a \times b) \times 10^{m+n}$  |  $(a \times 10^m) \div (b \times 10^n) = (a \div b) \times 10^{m-n}$  |  If $A \ge 10$, adjust it!
