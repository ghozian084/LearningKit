Hands-on 6 · Meeting 6: Peeling the Orange 🍊

Grade 9 · Topic 7 · Phase 1–3 · circles have radius 4 cm (for an orange about 8 cm across) · digital twin: Interactive Media 6

Steps: ➊ Check your orange: its diameter should match the circles (or draw your own circles with the orange's diameter). ➋ Peel the orange in small pieces. ➌ Fill circle 1, then 2, 3, 4 with peel. How many circles does the peel cover?
🧠 Conclusion

The peel covered about ____ circles. One circle has area $\pi r^2$, so the sphere's surface area $=$ ____ $\pi r^2$.

For $r = 4$ cm: surface area $=$ ________ cm²    Volume $= \frac43\pi r^3 =$ ________ cm³
