✂️ 📄 Paper-Folding Guide: $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4}$ ➊ Fold into 4 columns, shade 3 ($\frac{3}{4}$). ➋ Fold into 3 rows, shade 2 in another colour ($\frac{2}{3}$). ➌ Double-shaded boxes = top × top $= 2 \times 3 = 6$; all boxes = bottom × bottom $= 3 \times 4 = 12$. ➍ $\frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$ ✅
✂️🟢 Team 1 · Dietary Planners — Fractions

A healthy salad uses $1\frac{3}{4}$ cups of spinach for 4 people. How much spinach is needed to feed 18 people?

🔵 Team 2 · Epidemiologists — Fractions

A hospital has 480 beds and $\frac{5}{8}$ of them are occupied. 120 new (empty) beds are added. What fraction of the beds is occupied now?

🔴 Team 3 · Fitness Coaches — Fractions

A runner completes $3\frac{1}{2}$ laps of a track. Each lap is $\frac{2}{5}$ km. How far did the runner go?

🟡 Team 4 · Hydration Specialists — Fractions

A dispenser holds $15\frac{1}{2}$ L of water. Each bottle holds $\frac{3}{4}$ L. How many full bottles can be filled?
