Grade 9 · Topic 5 · EXPLORE phase — one set per student

How to use: Cut out the frog tokens. Place a frog where the decimal point is now and hop it one box at a time until only one non-zero digit is in front of it. Count the hops = $n$. SEN: highlight the digits of $A$ in a bright colour.
