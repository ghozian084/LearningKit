Meeting 2 · Explain Brackets & Fractions

Brackets $2(x - 4) = 14$

Expand first: $2x - 8 = 14 \Rightarrow 2x = 22 \Rightarrow x = 11$

Divide first: $x - 4 = 7 \Rightarrow x = 11$

Fractions Spot the difference!

$\dfrac{x}{3} + 5 = 9$:   $-5$ then $\times 3$ → $x = 12$

$\dfrac{x + 5}{3} = 9$:   $\times 3$ then $-5$ → $x = 22$

Meeting 3 · Explain Extracting the HCF

Method

  1. HCF of the numbers.
  2. HCF of the letters (lowest power).
  3. Write the HCF outside; divide each term by it.
  4. Expand to check!

Model

$6x^2 + 9x = 3x(2x + 3)$

Check: $3x \times 2x + 3x \times 3 = 6x^2 + 9x$ ✓

Meeting 5 · Explain Changing the Subject

Definition

The subject of a formula is the letter on its own, e.g. $y$ in $y = 3x - 4$.

Rule: use the EXACT SAME inverse operations as in solving equations!

Model Make $x$ the subject of $y = 3x - 4$

$y + 4 = 3x$  ⟹  $x = \dfrac{y + 4}{3}$

Scaffold: highlight the target letter!
