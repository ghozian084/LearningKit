⚡ PART A · Synthetic Division (linear divisors only)Meeting 4

How it works — divisor $(x - a)$: write $a$ on the left and the coefficients of $P(x)$ in order.

① Bring the first coefficient down. ② Multiply by $a$, write it under the next coefficient (↗). ③ Add. Repeat.

The last number is the remainder; the others are the coefficients of the quotient (one degree lower).

⚠️ Missing term? Write a 0 placeholder (e.g. $x^3 - 8 \to 1,\ 0,\ 0,\ -8$).

⚠️ Divisor $(cx - d)$? Use $a = \frac{d}{c}$, then divide the quotient coefficients by $c$ (the remainder stays the same).

Divide → Multiply → Subtract → Bring down, until the remainder's degree is less than the divisor's degree.

Quotient $Q(x) = x^2 + 3x - 2$

Remainder $R(x) = -4x - 1$ — a quadratic divisor can leave a linear remainder $ax + b$.

Check: $x^4 + 2x^3 - 3x^2 + 4x - 5$
