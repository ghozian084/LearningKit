When all three pieces are in place, the height of $P$ is made of two vertical pieces. Which ones?

$\cos(A + B) = OR - TQ =$ −

📝 Step-by-Step Proof

Step 1 (triangle ①): $OP = 1$ and $\angle QOP = B$, so $OQ = \cos B$ and $QP = \sin B$.
Step 2 (triangle ②): $OQ = \cos B$ and $\angle ROQ = A$, so $RQ = \cos B\sin A$ and $OR = \cos B\cos A$.
Step 3 (triangle ③): $QP = \sin B$ and $\angle TPQ = A$ (it equals $\angle ROQ$ because the sides are perpendicular), so $TP = \sin B\cos A$ and $TQ = \sin B\sin A$.
Step 4: $P$ is at angle $A + B$ on a circle of radius 1, so its height is $\sin(A + B)$ and its horizontal distance is $\cos(A + B)$.
Step 5 (height): $\sin(A + B) = RQ + TP = \sin A\cos B + \cos A\sin B$
Step 6 (width): $\cos(A + B) = OR - TQ = \cos A\cos B - \sin A\sin B$ $\blacksquare$
