Hands-on 6 · Meeting 6: "I Have… Who Has…?" Trigonometry Review Loop

Grade 12 · Topic 3 · Review before the Chapter 3 Evaluation — 20 cards (with fewer students, some hold two cards)

How to play: Shuffle and hand out all cards. The student with ⭐ START reads "Who has…?". The student whose card shows the answer says "I have…" and reads their own question. The loop ends back at START. Time the class, then play again to beat the record!
