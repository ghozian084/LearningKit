Part 1: Fraction Language (Mixed ↔ Improper)

🧠 Mindful Math Connection

Mixed → Improper: (whole × bottom) + top, over the same bottom.   $2\frac{3}{4} = \frac{2 \times 4 + 3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}$

Improper → Mixed: divide; the remainder becomes the new top.   $\frac{17}{5} = 3$ rem $2 = 3\frac{2}{5}$

Part 3: Multiplying & Dividing

🧠 Rules to Remember

Multiply: Top × Top, Bottom × Bottom.   Divide: Keep, Change, Flip ($\div \frac{a}{b} \rightarrow \times \frac{b}{a}$).   ⚠️ Mixed numbers → improper first!
