Hands-on 2 · Meeting 2: Assembly Mat & Formula Card

Place ② on the floor, ① on top of ②, and ③ in the gap under $P$. Then read the height of $P$.

🔎 Read the height of P: height $= RQ + TP = (\cos B)\sin A + (\sin B)\cos A$, so $\sin(A + B) = \sin A\cos B + \cos A\sin B$.   The horizontal distance $OR - TQ$ gives $\cos(A + B) = \cos A\cos B - \sin A\sin B$.
✂️ 📌 Formula card — glue in your notebook (signs that change are in red)
$\sin(A \pm B) \equiv \sin A\cos B \pm \cos A\sin B$
$\cos(A \pm B) \equiv \cos A\cos B$ ∓ $\sin A\sin B$
$\tan(A \pm B) \equiv \dfrac{\tan A \pm \tan B}{1 {\color{red}\mp} \tan A\tan B}$
Exact values: $\sin 45^\circ = \cos 45^\circ = \frac{\sqrt2}{2}$,   $\sin 30^\circ = \frac12$,   $\cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt3}{2}$,   $\tan 30^\circ = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}$,   $\tan 45^\circ = 1$
