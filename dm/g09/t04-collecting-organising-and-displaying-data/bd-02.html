The Semesta Healthy Lifestyles Dashboard 🏥

Project Briefing: Data Handling (Grade 9)

Are we as healthy as we think? Let the data speak!

1. The Mission Objective

Your Role Health Data Analysts

The Crisis: A recent study claims that 70% of teenagers are sleep deprived, dehydrated, and lack exercise. Is this true for our school?

Your Mission: We need local, primary data! You will collect real health metrics from your classmates, organize it, and build a massive visual dashboard.

The Outcome Presenting to the Principal

We will present this Dashboard during a Gallery Walk. We want to use mathematical evidence to advocate for better student health!

Example: If your data proves backpacks are too heavy, we can advocate for lockers!

2. The Rules of Data Collection

⚠️ The 3 Data Requirements

Your survey MUST contain exactly 3 questions. One for each data type:

  1. Categorical: Words/Choices (e.g., "What is your favorite snack?")
  2. Discrete: Counted numbers (e.g., "How many glasses of water?")
  3. Continuous: Measured numbers (e.g., "Exact hours of sleep?")

📋 The Golden Rule: Two-Way Tables

Whenever you ask a classmate a question, you MUST also record their Gender (Male / Female).

Why? Because later, you will need to create a Two-Way Table (e.g., Boys who play Futsal vs Girls who play Futsal).

Sample Size: Exactly 20 students per group.

4. Project Deliverables (What you will build)

The Dashboard Elements

Every group will be given an A3 Poster Board. It MUST include:

  • 1x Tally & Frequency Table (Grouped intervals).
  • 1x Two-Way Table.
  • 1x Stem-and-Leaf Diagram (Back-to-back if possible!).
  • 1x Bar Chart (Dual or Composite).
  • 1x Pictogram (With a clear Key).
  • 1x Pie Chart (Calculated accurately to 360°).

5. Timeline & Success Criteria

🗓️ The Timeline

  • Meeting 1 (Today): Pick groups & Design 3 Survey Questions.
  • Meeting 2: Leave desks. Collect data! Convert to tables.
  • Meeting 3: Build Stem-and-Leaf diagrams.
  • Meeting 4: Draw Bar Charts & Pictograms.
  • Meeting 5: Calculate & Draw Pie Charts. Assemble Dashboard.
  • Meeting 6: Gallery Walk & Chapter Exam.

🎯 How you will be graded

1. Mathematical Accuracy: Do your pie chart angles sum to 360°? Are your stems ordered?

2. Visual Rules: Do bar charts have equal gaps? Does the pictogram have a Key?

3. Collaboration: Did everyone in the group contribute to the data collection and drawing?
