💡 EXPLAIN · The Multiplier Method15 min
🧠 Complete the rules

➊ $r\%$ of a quantity: multiply by $\frac{r}{100}$.   15% of 2000 = × 2000

➋ Increase by $r\%$ → multiply by $1 + \frac{r}{100}$  |  Decrease by $r\%$ → multiply by

➌ $A$ as a percentage of $B$: $\frac{A}{B} \times 100\%$ — both in the units!

➍ $\text{Percentage change} = \dfrac{\text{New} - \text{Original}}{\text{Original}} \times 100\%$   (negative → decrease)
