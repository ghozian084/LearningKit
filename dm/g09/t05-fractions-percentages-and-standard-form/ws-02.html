Part 1: The Multiplier Method

🧠 Mindful Math Connection

🥤 "20% less sugar" means the new drink keeps $100\% - 20\% = 80\%$ of the sugar.   $0.80 \times 30\text{ g} = 24\text{ g}$.

Increase by $r\%$ → multiply by $1 + \frac{r}{100}$  |  Decrease by $r\%$ → multiply by $1 - \frac{r}{100}$

Part 3: The Micro & the Macro — Standard Form

🐸 Hands-On: Frog Jumps

Write the digits in the boxes. Move the decimal point so only one non-zero digit is in front of it. Draw a loop for each jump and count them — that is $n$.

Big number → $n$ is positive.   Tiny number (0.000…) → $n$ is negative.   Always $1 \le A < 10$.

Part 4: Calculating in Standard Form

🧠 Index Laws

$(a \times 10^m) \times (b \times 10^n) = (a \times b) \times 10^{m+n}$  |  $(a \times 10^m) \div (b \times 10^n) = (a \div b) \times 10^{m-n}$  |  If $A \ge 10$, adjust it!
