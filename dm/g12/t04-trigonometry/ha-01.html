Hands-on 1 · Meeting 1: Measuring the Unit Circle

Grade 12 · Topic 3 · EXPLORE phase — one sheet per student · ruler & calculator needed

Steps: ➊ Draw the tangent to the circle at $A$ (vertical line). ➋ Draw the tangent at $B$ (horizontal line). ➌ Extend $OP$ until it meets the tangent at $A$ at point $T$ and the tangent at $B$ at point $S$. ➍ Measure $AT$, $OT$, $BS$, $OS$ in cm and record them in Worksheet 1, Question 3.
✏️ Quick record (then copy to Worksheet 1)

$AT$ = ______ cm    $OT$ = ______ cm    $BS$ = ______ cm    $OS$ = ______ cm    (divide each by 7)

Which length is longer than the radius for every acute angle: $OT$, $OS$, both? ______   So $\sec\theta \ge$ ____ and $\operatorname{cosec}\theta \ge$ ____ when $0^\circ < \theta < 90^\circ$.
