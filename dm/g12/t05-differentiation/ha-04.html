🔎 Case 1: find $\frac{dy}{dx}$ for $xy + y^2 = 6$
①  $\frac{d}{dx}(xy) + \frac{d}{dx}(y^2) = 0$
②  $\frac{dy}{dx} + 2y\frac{dy}{dx} = 0$
③  $\frac{dy}{dx}(1 + 2y) = 0$
④  $\frac{dy}{dx} = 0$

③  $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{2x + 3y}{3x + 2y}$
