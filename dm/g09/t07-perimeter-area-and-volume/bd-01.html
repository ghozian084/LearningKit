Meeting 1 · Stimulation Fence or Fertiliser?

Sawah 🌾

A farmer wants to put a fence around the sawah, and buy fertiliser to cover the inside soil.

What is the difference between these two measurements? Which units?

Problem Statement

We only know Area of a rectangle $= L \times W$. How can we prove the area formulas of a parallelogram and a triangle?

1D vs 2D

Meeting 1 · Data One Cut, Then Slide

Hands-on 1 ✂️ (or Interactive Media 1)

  • Make exactly ONE cut to turn the parallelogram into a rectangle.
  • Join two identical triangles. What shape appears?
  • Count squares before and after.

Processing

Parallelogram $= b \times h$    Triangle $= \frac12 b \times h$

$h$ = perpendicular height, not the slant side!

Draw the Cut

Meeting 3 · Stimulation Rim or Cover?

Tampah 🧺

A round tampah has diameter 50 cm. We need rattan around the rim and a banana leaf to cover it.

Which is a perimeter? Which is an area? Which units?

Problem Statement

A circle has no straight sides. How do we measure around it — and inside it?

Ideas

Meeting 3 · Data Hunt for π & the Pizza

Hands-on 3 🧵 (or Interactive Media 3)

String around circles A, B, C ($d$ = 4, 6, 8 cm) and your own objects. Record $C \div d$.

$C \div d \approx 3.14 = \pi$  ⇒  $C = \pi d = 2\pi r$

Processing 🍕

16 sectors top-to-tail ≈ rectangle: height $r$, base $\frac12 \times 2\pi r = \pi r$

$A = \pi r \times r = \pi r^2$

Our Table

Meeting 3 · Verification & Generalization

Worksheet 3

Sector with angle $\theta$ = the fraction $\frac{\theta}{360}$ of the circle:

arc $= \frac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$    area $= \frac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$

Semicircle $d = 12$: perimeter $= 6\pi + 12 \approx 30.8$ cm (don’t forget the straight edge!)

Exit Ticket

Tampah, $d = 50$ cm: rattan for the rim? Banana leaf to cover it?

Meeting 6 · Stimulation Skin or Meat?

Volume

Surface area is the "skin". Volume is the "meat". A prism is its base shape stacked to a height:

$V = \text{Area of base} \times \text{height}$

Problem Statement

A pyramid has the same base and height as a prism. What fraction of the prism does it fill?

Predictions

Meeting 6 · Data The Rice-Pouring Experiment

Hands-on 6 🍚 (or Interactive Media 6)

Open prism and open pyramid: square base 5 cm, height 6 cm.

Fill the pyramid, pour it into the prism. How many pours?

Discovery

It takes exactly 3! → Pyramid / cone: $V = \frac13 \times \text{base area} \times h$

Cylinder $V = \pi r^2 h$   Cone $V = \frac13 \pi r^2 h$

Record the Pours

Worksheet 6

Exit Ticket

Meeting 7 · Stimulation Peeling the Orange 🍊

Hands-on 7 (or Interactive Media 7)

Draw 4 circles with the orange's diameter. Peel the orange — the peel fills exactly 4 circles!

Sphere: SA $= 4\pi r^2$    $V = \frac43 \pi r^3$

Synthesis

1D: cm (perimeter) · 2D: cm² (area) · 3D: cm³ (volume) — then the "I have… who has…?" loop.
