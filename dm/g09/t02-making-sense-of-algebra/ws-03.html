1. Basic Concept: Building a Formula

A formula uses an equals sign ($=$) to link variables together as a rule.
In the real world, costs often have a fixed part and a changing part:
Total ($C$) = (Fixed Constant) + (Variable Rate $\times$ Quantity)

2. Example Question and Solution

Question: A plumber charges a call-out fee of Rp 50,000, plus Rp 20,000 for every hour ($h$) he works. Write a formula for his total charge ($C$).

Solution: The fixed constant is 50,000. The variable rate is 20,000 multiplied by $h$.
Answer: $C = 50000 + 20000h$.

1. Basic Concept: Substitution

Substitution means replacing a letter with a specific number.
Golden Rule: ALWAYS put the number in brackets when substituting, especially if it is negative! Example: If $x = -3$, then $x^2 \rightarrow (-3)^2 = 9$.

2. Example Question and Solution

Question: Evaluate $3a - b^2$ when $a = 4$ and $b = -2$.

Solution: Substitute with brackets: $3(4) - (-2)^2$
Calculate powers first: $(-2)^2 = 4$
Multiply: $12 - 4$
Answer: $8$.

1. Basic Concept: Like Terms

Like Terms must have the EXACT same letters and powers. ($x$ and $x^2$ are different!).
You can only add or subtract like terms. The sign ($+$ or $-$) belongs to the term immediately to its right.

2. Example Question and Solution

Question: Simplify $5a - 2b + 3a + 5b$.

Solution: Group the $a$ terms and $b$ terms together WITH their signs.
$a$ terms: $5a + 3a = 8a$
$b$ terms: $-2b + 5b = +3b$
Answer: $8a + 3b$.

Expanding means multiplying the term outside the bracket by EVERYTHING inside the bracket. $a(b + c) = ab + ac$.

Worksheet M6: Double Brackets
Grade 9 - Making Sense of Algebra
Name:
Class:
1. Basic Concept: Box Method & FOIL

When expanding two brackets $(x+a)(x+b)$, every term in the first bracket must multiply every term in the second bracket.
FOIL: First, Outer, Inner, Last.
Then, collect the like terms in the middle!

2. Example Question and Solution

Question: Expand $(x + 2)(x + 4)$.

Solution:
First: $x \times x = x^2$ | Outer: $x \times 4 = 4x$ | Inner: $2 \times x = 2x$ | Last: $2 \times 4 = 8$
Result: $x^2 + 4x + 2x + 8$. Combine middle terms: $x^2 + 6x + 8$.

4. Case: Perfect Squares

Be careful with $(x - 5)^2$. It does NOT equal $x^2 - 25$! A power of 2 means the entire bracket is multiplied by itself: $(x - 5)(x - 5)$. You must expand all 4 terms.

Grade 9 - Making Sense of Algebra
Name:

1. Basic Concept: Zero, Negative & Fractional Indices

Zero Index: Anything to the power of 0 equals 1. ($x^0 = 1$)
Negative Index: Creates a fraction. Flips the base. ($x^{-n} = \frac{1}{x^n}$)
Fractional Index: Represents a root. ($x^{1/2} = \sqrt{x}$)

2. Example Question and Solution

Question: Evaluate $3^{-2}$ and $25^{1/2}$.

Solution:
$3^{-2} = \frac{1}{3^2} = \frac{1}{9}$.
$25^{1/2} = \sqrt{25} = 5$.

4. Case: Fractional Powers on Big Numbers

If you see a fractional index like $144^{1/2}$, it simply means you are looking for the square root of 144. Just ask yourself: "What number multiplied by itself makes 144?"

Worksheet M8: Rules of Indices
Grade 9 - Making Sense of Algebra
Name:
Class:
1. Basic Concept: The 3 Laws

Multiply: Add the powers $\rightarrow a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$
Divide: Subtract the powers $\rightarrow \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$
Power of Power: Multiply the powers $\rightarrow (a^m)^n = a^{mn}$

2. Example Question and Solution

Question: Simplify $x^4 \times x^5$.

Solution: Since we are multiplying the bases, we ADD the indices. $4 + 5 = 9$.
Answer: $x^9$.

In algebra, it is critical to know what operation you are performing. Are you Substituting (plugging numbers in)? Expanding (removing brackets)? Simplifying (collecting like terms)? Or applying Index Laws?
