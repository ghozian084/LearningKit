Meeting 1: Logarithms to Base 10 (Part 1)

Main Concept The Inverse Function

  • Addition undoes subtraction. Roots undo powers. But what undoes the exponent itself?
  • Definition: $y = \log_{10} x \iff 10^y = x$
  • The logarithm finds the power needed to reach a number!
  • Notation: $\log_{10}$ is often just written as $\lg$ or $\log$.

Activity The Richter Scale

Hook: "An earthquake in Indonesia measures 5.0. Another measures 6.0. Is a 6.0 just slightly stronger?"

Task: Let's look at the math. It's actually $10^5$ vs $10^6$. That means a 6.0 is exactly 10 TIMES stronger! This is a Logarithmic Scale.

Meeting 2: Logarithms to Base $a$ (Part 1)

Main Concept General Base Logarithms

  • We are not restricted to base 10!
  • Rule: $y = \log_a x \iff a^y = x$
  • Special Values: $\log_a a = 1$ and $\log_a 1 = 0$.

Activity Computer Binary

Hook: "Computers operate on base 2. If computer memory doubles every generation, we use $2^x$. How do we find x?"

Task: By trial and error, find the value of $x$ for $2^x = 32$. Write it in logarithmic notation!

Meeting 3: The Laws of Logarithms (Part 1)

Main Concept The 3 Laws

  • Product Law: $\log_a (xy) = \log_a x + \log_a y$
  • Quotient Law: $\log_a (x/y) = \log_a x - \log_a y$
  • Power Law: $\log_a (x^n) = n \log_a x$
  • Trap: $\log(A+B)$ is NOT $\log A + \log B$!

Activity Napier's Bones

Hook: "Before calculators, astronomers multiplied 8-digit numbers by ADDING their logarithms on a slide rule. How?"

Task: Calculate $\log_{10}(100) + \log_{10}(1000)$. Now calculate $\log_{10}(100 \times 1000)$. What do you notice?

Meeting 4/5: Solving Log Equations & Change of Base (Part 1)

Main Concept Equation Strategy

  • Step 1: Use the Log Laws to compress everything into ONE single logarithm.
  • Step 2: Convert from Logarithmic Form into Exponential Form.
  • Step 3: Solve the resulting algebraic equation.
  • Step 4: Check for extraneous roots! You cannot log a negative number.

Activity Hidden Quadratics

Hook: "Solve $\log_3 x + \log_3(x-2) = 1$."

Task: In your groups, combine the logs. Convert to exponent form. You will get a quadratic! Solve it, and test your roots.

Meeting 6/7: Exponential Equations & Inequalities (Part 1)

Main Concept Bringing the Power Down

  • To solve $a^x = b$, take the $\log$ (or $\ln$) of BOTH sides.
  • Use the Power Law to bring $x$ down: $x \log a = \log b$.
  • For Inequalities: If you divide by a $\log$ of a number less than 1 (like $\log 0.5$), the value is negative. You MUST FLIP the inequality sign!

Activity The Flipping Sign

Hook: "Solve $0.5^x < 10$."

Task: Take the log of both sides. What is the value of $\log(0.5)$ in your calculator? It's negative! Solve the inequality carefully.

Meeting 8: Natural Logarithms (Part 1)

Main Concept Euler's Number ($e$)

  • $e \approx 2.718$ is the base of natural, continuous growth.
  • The "Natural Logarithm" is $\log_e x$, written simply as $\mathbf{\ln x}$.
  • $\ln(e) = 1$, and $\ln(e^x) = x$.

Activity Continuous Compounding

Hook: "An Indonesian bank offers 100% interest yearly. If compounded monthly? Daily? Every second?"

Task: It doesn't approach infinity! It approaches the magical limit $e$. This is why radioactive decay and cooling use $e$.

Meetings 9-12: PjBL - Linear Forms (Part 1)

Main Concept Transforming Curves to Lines

  • Non-linear models like $y = A b^x$ are hard to graph and analyze.
  • Take the log of both sides: $\log y = \log A + x \log b$.
  • Compare to a straight line: $Y = mX + c$.
  • Here, $Y = \log y$, the gradient $m = \log b$, and the y-intercept $c = \log A$!

Activity The Cooling Curve Project

Hook: "How fast does your Teh Panas cool down? Can we predict the exact temperature in 2 hours?"

Task: You will collect real temperature data over 40 mins, plot $\ln y$ vs $t$, find the gradient, and determine the exact cooling constants $A$ and $k$ for your specific cup!
