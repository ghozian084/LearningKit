Meeting 1 · Explain The Fraction Rules

Language Types of Fractions

Proper $\frac{3}{4}$   Improper $\frac{7}{4}$   Mixed $1\frac{3}{4}$   Equivalent $\frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4}$

$2\frac{3}{4} = \frac{2 \times 4 + 3}{4} = \frac{11}{4}$  |  $\frac{17}{5} = 3\frac{2}{5}$

Operations

  • $+$ / $-$: common denominator first. $\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{3}{6} + \frac{2}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$
  • $\times$: top × top, bottom × bottom. $\frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{4} = \frac{6}{12} = \frac{1}{2}$
  • $\div$: Keep, Change, Flip. $\frac{3}{4} \div \frac{3}{8} = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{8}{3} = 2$
  • ⚠️ Mixed numbers → improper fractions first!
Guided Practice: $2\frac{1}{4} + 1\frac{2}{3}$

Meeting 2 · Explain The Multiplier Method

Rules

  • $r\%$ of a quantity → multiply by $\frac{r}{100}$:   15% of 2000 = $0.15 \times 2000 = 300$
  • Increase $r\%$ → $\times\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)$;   Decrease $r\%$ → $\times\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right)$
  • $A$ as a % of $B$: $\frac{A}{B} \times 100\%$ (same units!)
  • $\text{Percentage change} = \dfrac{\text{New} - \text{Original}}{\text{Original}} \times 100\%$

Meeting 3 · Explain $A \times 10^n$ & Index Laws

Standard Form $A \times 10^n$,  $1 \le A < 10$

$275\,000\,000 = 2.75 \times 10^{8}$  (8 hops → $n = +8$)

$0.000\,000\,12 = 1.2 \times 10^{-7}$  (7 hops → $n = -7$)

Big numbers → $n$ positive; tiny numbers → $n$ negative. (= Scientific Notation)

Index Laws

$(2 \times 10^3) \times (4 \times 10^5) = 8 \times 10^{8}$

$(6 \times 10^8) \div (3 \times 10^2) = 2 \times 10^{6}$

$(9 \times 10^4)(5 \times 10^3) = 45 \times 10^7 = 4.5 \times 10^8$ ⚠️ fix $A$!
