🧠 My proof toolbox — complete each identity

➊ Start from the side and work towards the other side. Never move terms across "≡".

➋ Rewrite in $\sin$ and $\cos$:   $\tan\theta \equiv$   $\sec\theta \equiv$   $\operatorname{cosec}\theta \equiv$   $\cot\theta \equiv$

➌ Pythagorean:   $\sin^2\theta + \cos^2\theta \equiv$   $1 + \tan^2\theta \equiv$   $1 + \cot^2\theta \equiv$

➍ Double angle:   $\sin 2\theta \equiv$   $\cos 2\theta \equiv$ (3 forms)

➎ Algebra: combine fractions over a common denominator; factorise, e.g. $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$.
