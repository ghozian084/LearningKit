💡 EXPLAIN · Extracting the HCF15 min
🧠 Method

➊ HCF of the numbers.   ➋ HCF of each letter = its lowest power.   ➌ Write the HCF outside the bracket; divide each term by it.   ➍ Expand to check!

Example: $6x^2 + 9x$ → numbers: HCF(6, 9) = 3; letters: $x^2$ and $x$ → $x$   ⇒   $6x^2 + 9x = 3x(2x + 3)$ ✓
