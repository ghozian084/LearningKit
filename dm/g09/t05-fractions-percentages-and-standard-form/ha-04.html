Hands-on 4 · Meeting 4: "I Have… Who Has…?" Review Loop

Grade 9 · Topic 5 · Review before the Chapter 5 Evaluation — 20 cards = exactly one card for each of the 20 students

How to play: Shuffle and hand out all 20 cards. The student with ⭐ START reads "Who has…?". The student whose card shows the answer says "I have…" and reads their own question. The loop ends back at the START card. Time the class, then play again to beat the record!
